What is FTP? ⬇

Your maximum power output you can hold for one hour. Use about 95% of your best 20-minute power as a rough guide.

What is IF? ⬇

How hard you plan to ride as a percentage of your FTP. 0.85 means 85% of your max — a solid race effort for most people.

Enter the upper limit of each heart rate zone (bpm)

What are HR zones? ⬇

Heart rate zones are effort levels. Zone 1 is very easy, Zone 4 is very hard. For a long race, try to stay in Zone 2–3 for most of it.

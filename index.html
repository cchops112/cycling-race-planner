What is FTP? ⬇

FTP = max 1-hour power. Use ~95% of 20-min effort.

What is IF? ⬇

IF = effort vs FTP. 0.85 = 85% FTP.

Enter the upper limit of each heart rate zone (bpm)

What are HR zones? ⬇

Zones are effort levels based on heart rate. Zone 1 = very easy, Zone 4 = very hard. Stay in Zone 2–3 for most of a long race.
